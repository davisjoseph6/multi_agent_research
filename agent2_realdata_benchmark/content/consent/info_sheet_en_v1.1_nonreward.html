Participant information sheet — version v1.1

Study title: Self-Healing Adaptive Quiz – Evaluation with Real Learners. Information sheet version: v1.1, [date].
 Who runs the study?          aivancity School of AI & Data for Business & Society, Villejuif, France (data controller).
                              Principal investigator: Antoun Yaacoub, Associate Professor ([email]). Team: Davis
                              [surname], Sara [surname], Wiam [surname], aivancity students.
 Why this study?              To find out whether a quiz that detects when a learner struggles, and offers targeted help,
                              improves learning compared with other ways of running a quiz. The results will be
                              published in scientific conferences or journals.
 What will you do?            3 online sessions of 45 minutes, in English, 3 to 5 days apart: an initial test (16
                              questions), quizzes (20, 20, then 15 questions), a final test (16 questions) and a short
                              questionnaire after each session. The quiz may offer you help: short texts, worked
                              examples, guided questions.
 Groups                       There are several versions of the quiz. One is assigned to you automatically. You will not
                              know which one during the study, so that it does not influence your answers; it is
                              explained to you at the end.
 Who can take part?           Any aivancity student aged 18 or over who has never taken a course in economics or
                              behavioral science.
 Voluntary participation      You are free to accept or refuse. Your decision has no effect on your grades, your studies
                              or your relationship with the school. Your teachers do not know who takes part.
 Risks and benefits           No foreseeable risk, apart from possible tiredness. You can take a break at any time.
                              Benefits: discovering behavioral economics and contributing to student research in
                              educational AI.
 Participation incentive      AI points are not offered for this invitation, as the registered email is outside
                              @aivancity.education. Participation is voluntary and has no effect on grades.
 Data collected               Your email (only to send you the link and reminders); your age and study program; your
                              answers to the eligibility questions; for each question: the answer chosen, whether it is
                              correct, and the response time; the text you type in the guided questions; your answers to
                              the workload questionnaire; login dates and times. We do not collect your name or any
                              sensitive data.
 Legal basis                  Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
 Pseudonymization             Your answers are stored under a code (for example P024). The link between this code
                              and your email is kept in a separate table, accessible only to the principal investigator
                              and the two people who send the invitations. It is deleted when data collection ends.
 Automated decisions          The quiz automatically chooses questions and help. These choices have no legal effect
                              and no effect on your studies.
 Who can access the data?     Only the research team. The data is hosted in the European Union ([host]). It is never
                              sold or shared with third parties. The help content is pre-written: none of your answers is
                              sent to an external AI service.
 Retention                    Email and link table: deleted when data collection ends, at the latest on [date].
                              Pseudonymized data: kept for 5 years after the last publication, then deleted. If you agree
                              (optional box), a fully anonymized dataset may be published so that other researchers
                              can check the results.
 Your rights                  Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. Write to
                              [principal investigator email] or to aivancity’s DPO: [DPO email], giving the email you
                              registered with. Once the link table is deleted, your data can no longer be linked to you
                              and cannot be erased individually. You can also complain to the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
 Stopping                     You can stop at any time without giving a reason: simply stop logging in, or write to
                              [email]. On request, your data is deleted as long as the link table exists. Otherwise, the
                              data already collected is kept and used.
 Contact                      [Name, principal investigator email] – DPO: [DPO email]
