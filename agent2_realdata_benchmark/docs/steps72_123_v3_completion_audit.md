## Why all 52 cannot be marked complete here

1. A fabricated question with `expert_reviewed` status is not evidence of
   actual independent educational review.
2. Synthetic pilot data cannot establish psychometric reliability, learning
   efficacy, causal effects on humans, or generalizability.
3. The original IJCAI manuscript's global recovery theorem cannot be derived
   from this uncalibrated model; its claim requires a separate formal audit.
4. An AI-generated informed-consent document cannot replace institutional
   sponsorship, privacy assessment, ethics determination, or human oversight.
5. This development browser is deliberately loopback-only and refuses
   non-synthetic participant IDs; it cannot operate as a real RCT system.

The right release label is: **eight-concept synthetic experimental engine,
with opt-in POMCP integration and functional developer browser — not approved
for human testing.**
