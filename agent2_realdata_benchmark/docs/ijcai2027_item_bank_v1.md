## Research limitations

No claim is made that this is a validated item bank.

The Bloom classifications are provisional.

The candidate independent probe has not been
validated for difficulty, discrimination, or
measurement independence.

Only one of the proposed eight concepts is represented.

The current API retains its two-question development
workflow and is not a participant-study application.
