## Stop-ship scientific decisions

The following are **unresolved**, not automatically resolved by 1,388 passing WSL tests or the FIFO performance experiment:

1. **Final interventions and scientific claims** (SUP-01..05): what exactly constitutes A, B and C; whether BKT/Bloom and fixed recovery sequences remain required, what is meant by “self-healing”, and whether NN/BKT/DKT results are mandatory.
2. **Validated measurement instrument and timing** (SUP-10..11): NASA-TLX six ratings after sessions **versus** six total digital M-VAS ratings (one pre and one post per session) must receive a signed choice; the existing deployed fictional six-item questionnaire must not be relabeled.
3. **Randomization and recruitment design** (SUP-08..09,12..14): concealment, consent, GDPR, population eligibility, room/staffing plan and justified cohort size require PI/statistician/institutional signoff.
4. **Independent content, technology and QA** (SUP-04,06..07): all 95 provisional items and 32 resources, source rights, translations, interface, authorized production database and independent accessibility/security/HTTP/load/recovery review remain unsigned.

## Required evidence and ownership

- **PI and statistician:** adjudicate all scientific choices above, date and sign the final protocol + SAP + sample size, own amendment ledger and research questions; independent statistician confirms contrasts, attrition and fatigue hypotheses.
- **Measurement/pedagogy reviewers:** approve exact fatigue instrument and scoring; review item IDs, distractors, Bloom levels, translations and resources with hashes.
- **Responsible institution, ethics and DPO:** determine research category/approval pathway, legal basis and retention, secure approved collection/hosting, appropriate language/consent, recruitment and compensation procedures.
- **Engineering + independent QA:** version source and content, independently test concealed allocation and real scheduling, staff roles, browser/HTTPS/device/accessibility/security, load with realistic peak simultaneous use, incident/backups with signed GO evidence.
